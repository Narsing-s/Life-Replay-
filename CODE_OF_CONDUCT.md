# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We are committed to making Life Replay a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free open-source project for everyone.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Assume good intent and discuss technical disagreements professionally.
- Give useful, actionable feedback.
- Respect privacy, especially when discussing personal memories, photos, locations, or account data.
- Welcome contributors with different backgrounds and experience levels.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, doxxing, sexual harassment, hateful conduct, and deliberate disruption are not acceptable.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, open a private security/contact report through the repository owner rather than posting sensitive information publicly in an issue.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove comments, close discussions, reject contributions, or restrict participation when necessary to protect the community.

## Scope

This code of conduct applies to project spaces and interactions where participants represent or participate in the Life Replay community.
